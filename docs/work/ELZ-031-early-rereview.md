### A6 comparison and verifiable terms

The SIX [terms of use](https://www.six-group.com/en/services/legal/terms-and-conditions/terms-of-use.html), retrieved in this review, say information on the website is provided “exclusively for personal use as well as information purposes”; they state “The entire content of the SIX website is protected by copyright law” and “Consequently, presentations, brochures, flyers, graphics, texts, designs, charts, etc., may not be reproduced or reused in any way or used for commercial purposes.” These are terms as published; they do not on their face identify these specific ISO files or resolve the rights in a derived factual extract. No applicable permission for the list files was found or demonstrated in the reviewed materials; no legal conclusion is asserted. AGENTS.md says Apache-2.0 covers Elzoo-owned source and upstream licenses must be recorded separately; `docs/development/08-upstream-and-forks.md` requires verification of the exact version, affected files and applicable terms.

1. **Verbatim XML:** preserves test evidence against the external dated source and matches the existing Scope, but obtain applicable reproduction/redistribution permission or a suitable license before committing. Provenance and hashes are not permission, and owner's acceptance of risk is not a license from SIX.
2. **Derived extract and source hashes:** avoids committing verbatim XML and can support reproducible drift checks, but the extract itself still requires rights/terms assessment; hashes alone do not establish rights or prove faithful extraction without a verifiable source comparison. It changes the Scope's `an externally sourced, dated dataset committed beside the test` requirement, and comparison against a derived extract is weaker than direct comparison against the original. Proposed replacement: `an externally sourced, dated dataset is used to create a source-attributed, dated derived extract committed beside the test; source URLs, source hashes and a reproducible derivation and comparison method are recorded`, subject to coordinator review and rights determination.
