## Findings

1. **BLOCKING — Scope and publication ownership.** The proposal plans an edit to `registry/catalogue.json`, which is outside this dispatch's allowed paths. `docs/development/02-task-dag.md` says the coordinator serializes registry integration and regenerates the ledger after source merges; no shared file may be changed by the worker under this scope. Route the exact required row change to the coordinator or obtain an explicit scope amendment before it is edited. The already present claim marker is permitted by the claim protocol and must be removed before PR, not merged.

2. **BLOCKING — The proposed result and cleanup behavior contradict the live Contract.** The task requires waiting the grace period before escalating, cleanup even if the owning BEAM process dies, and timeout/collector-death tests leaving no surviving group. The proposed `grace --port exit--> check` can escalate while grace remains; `terminated{group: alive}` after KILL and an immediate `unknown{collector_exit}` while a reaper starts do not establish group death before the Adapter returns. A signal/check failure also ends the proposed machine without a specified independent cleanup attempt. Adapter-death handling assumes a collector already has the PGID: open-to-handover and collector/reaper/adapter concurrent death, Port-close behavior, and repeated cancellation must be accounted for. Provide a concrete owner/reaper handoff and testable lifetime/accounting design that meets the existing Contract, including when no observation can be obtained; do not present an unverified residual as Contract-compliant. If that cannot be done, stop and seek a reviewed Contract decision; do not weaken the law or acceptance silently. Whole-VM death and escaped groups must be precisely separated from BEAM-owner death.

3. **BLOCKING — `kill` status is not observation of the claimed facts.** The proposal equates utility exit 0 with signal delivery to every member, and exit 1 with ESRCH while admitting EPERM as an assumption. A utility's success is not proof that every member died; an error status does not by itself distinguish permission, bad invocation and absence. In particular fake `kill` returning 0 while delivering nothing is a named task danger and is not in the tests; an exit-2 script may fail on the first signal rather than the intended liveness check. A real independent liveness observation and injected failure controls must distinguish actually observed dead from unknown, never report no-process on an ambiguous failure. Bound PID/PGID reuse risks: after reaping/leader exit, do not signal an unrelated reused group; specify the OS and lifetime assumptions and prove the mitigation on the supported runner, or stop for a reviewed design.

4. **BLOCKING — shared result mismatch and proposed new variants.** Live #46 says timed-out/dispatched carries termination stage; ready `core/envelope` explicitly rejects arbitrary extra error keys and value/schema fields on non-completed outcomes. The proposal keeps stage only in the Adapter result, so the stated mapping does not carry it. Also `rejected{spawn_failed | cancelled}` and cancellation/caller-down outcomes extend the published result set; asynchronous spawn failure cannot generally be returned synchronously from `submit/3` after queueing. Use an explicitly paired Adapter-result sidecar or another reviewed composition that preserves stage without changing the ready Envelope Contract. The coordinator should review these exact issue-copy amendments before such variants are implemented; these changes add specificity without weakening existing laws:

   - Before: `rejected{queue_full | queue_timeout}` (not dispatched). After: `rejected{queue_full | queue_timeout | spawn_failed | cancelled}` (not dispatched; `spawn_failed` only where no command was dispatched; `cancelled` only where a queued command was not dispatched).
   - Before: `terminated` on deadline → `timed_out/dispatched` carrying the stage. After: `terminated` on deadline → `timed_out/dispatched`, paired with the Adapter result carrying the observed stage; the shared delivery-result map remains unchanged and has no stage field.
   - Add after that mapping: `terminated` on explicit cancellation or caller death → `failed/dispatched`, paired with the Adapter result carrying the observed stage; only report definitive termination when it is observed, and partial external Effects remain possible.

   Preserve exact existing acceptance, group-death and observed-stage requirements. `core/envelope` requires correlation_id defaulting to request message_id, and its validator checks shape, not the payload schema, identity truth, or cleanup.

5. **BLOCKING — Needs and environment conflict with decision record.** The decision-ready record calls for declared executable versions, working directory and environment allowlist. Proposal instead inherits the entire VM environment by default, permitting credentials to enter CLI subprocesses, and leaves exact CLI versions open. Either implement/document an explicit allowlist and record executable versions/operational Needs at the agreed owning task, or obtain the owner's reviewed decision changing the record. Do not claim the record and proposal already agree. No need to add an Elixir SDK, copy Jido, or claim sandboxing. OTP Port at OTP 29.1.2 is the selected provider, not a separately versioned process library; the decision record explicitly rejects `jido_shell`/`jido_workspace` and notes its process-library survey was not freshly rechecked.

6. **REQUIRED — deadline and grace semantics.** `delivery/retry-timeout` converts the absolute Envelope deadline at admission to a local monotonic target. The proposal's required fresh `timeout_ms` beginning at spawn can extend the original deadline by queue residence; it does not explain expiry while queued or timer races. Define how remaining deadline is calculated at admission and dispatch, expired-before-dispatch classification, queue-timeout ordering, late timer messages, and bounded timer intervals. Assert queue delay cannot restart an absolute deadline. A caller-supplied relative spawn timeout may be a separate setting, but cannot silently replace the declared deadline.

7. **REQUIRED — evidence gaps and upstream precision.** Add tests for escaped double-fork as an explicitly isolated, safely cleaned residual; fake successful non-delivery; failed/ambiguous liveness and signal checks; early Port exit versus grace; group cleanup when stdout is closed; Adapter/collector handoff and failure races; concurrent capacity conservation, bounded queue/release, late messages and duplicate cancels; no output leakage via telemetry; stage propagation sidecar; and a copied-source consumer without root Application. Run the task-required tests on the Linux CI runner and identify its actual OS. The proposal correctly separates finite tests, upstream and assumptions and names no proof (Q3: no new Cure law is mandated), but `erl_child_setup.c` implementation details and locally observed EOF/exit conventions are pinned implementation observations, not documented stable upstream API promises. Verify exact `kill` behavior for the supported environment; do not turn BSD and procps observations into a cross-platform contract. A `ps` group-leader test and kill-0 group probe are finite observations, not proofs of universal cleanup.

8. **REQUIRED — operational declarations and direct dependencies.** Proposal says no events and fixes configuration at construction/per call, but does not specify actual `piece.json` Telemetry/Config/Control statuses and contracts. Declare all three, with in-bundle test evidence for supported settings and control, including cancellation authority, preconditions, operation identity, duplicate behavior, accepted/applied/unknown, and no drain guarantee. Exclude telemetry explicitly or classify planned as appropriate; avoid leaking command, args, environment or output. The decision record lists `core/effect` and `runtime/supervision`; proposed direct dependencies are backpressure and envelope. It is reasonable not to copy an unused supervision recipe or effect declaration merely to name it, but the mismatch needs an explicit coordinator disposition: say which are actual source dependencies and which are caller-side composition/Needs, without misrepresenting record or omitting any consumed source. The ready backpressure Piece is a pure zero-queue counter; the proposed FIFO and its concurrency, timing and resource guarantees are new Adapter-owned policy, not established by the three selected backpressure helper proofs.

9. **LOW — completion and Q4.** Exit+EOF can be an implementable declared completion condition if it is tested and the coordinator accepts the consequence; it must not weaken group-reap or deadline acceptance. A child that exits with a remaining member holding stdout, and a member that closes stdout, are distinct cases. Record output truncation, inherited stderr exposure and stdin behavior as limits; `:inherit` can expose secrets to the VM's stderr.
