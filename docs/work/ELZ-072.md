# ELZ-072 — http-job: the job-control test counts provider sends, not polls

Task copy: `tasks/ELZ-072-http-job-test-sends.md` (issue #133). Test-only fix-forward; owner approval of the merged-test change on 2026-11-09 at 09:36 WIB, relayed by the main coordinator.

## Reason

The fixture in `pieces/assembly/http-job/test/support/http_job_test_helper.exs` records every request as `{method, path, bytes}`. The merged test at `pieces/assembly/http-job/test/elzoo/http_job_test.exs:485` asserted `length(Fixture.calls(c.f)) == 1`. When the `PUT` (send plus receipt authentication) exceeds the Adapter's 250 ms total budget under CPU starvation, the Adapter returns `{:error, :transport_timeout, :unknown}`, the binding maps it to an unknown dispatch, the owner stays in the dispatched phase, and the next Oban perform polls with one `GET`; the fixture answers `ack: true`, the owner reaches `{:Confirmed, 1}`, and the job completes inside the test's wait. The Adapter and owner behave as the Contract requires: an unknown dispatch is resolved by a poll, never by a second send. A poll is therefore a permitted observation after a slow `PUT`, and the assertion now counts `PUT` calls only:

    assert Enum.count(Fixture.calls(c.f), &match?({"PUT", _, _}, &1)) == 1
