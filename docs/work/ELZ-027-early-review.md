## Findings

**BLOCKING 1 — Offline classification changes the live Contract.** The issue says slots passed while offline, described as those between last recorded instant and now, become occurrences only under queue. The proposal's elapsed-time grace admits such a slot under skip or replace whenever it falls within grace, even if it was missed during downtime; elapsed lateness cannot distinguish offline from live. Conversely a live slot can be called a misfire just for exceeding grace. The issue does not define an offline/live discriminator, and its parenthetical can also encompass ordinary already-due live slots, so do not implement a private interpretation or weaken the law. The following proposed issue amendment preserves the offline-only queue restriction and makes the missing distinction an explicit caller obligation; the main coordinator must obtain deliberate Contract review/approval and sync the task copy before implementation:

Before (issue, Interface and invariant):
> due slots that passed while offline (those between the last recorded instant and now) become occurrences only under `queue`

After (proposed exact replacement):
> The caller identifies each due slot passed while the scheduler was offline as an offline catch-up slot, using its retained last recorded instant and availability boundary; an offline catch-up slot becomes an occurrence only under `queue`, subject to its bound. Slots identified as live use the declared overlap policy. The caller supplies the availability boundary; elapsed time or a grace period alone does not establish whether a slot was offline.

This adds a caller-supplied distinction rather than assuming a clock can infer uptime. If a grace policy is desired in addition, write and review its admissible inputs and behavior separately; it cannot override this offline guarantee. Failure to establish the required availability boundary needs an explicit fail-closed or otherwise reviewed outcome, not silent classification.

**BLOCKING 2 — The selected laws do not prove the issue's per-policy bound over the actual pure transition.** The ten proposed laws reason about `slot_step` supplied arbitrary preselected decisions; `fresh_adds_one` even permits a Fresh decision at full capacity. A buggy classifier can select Fresh when full while every proposed bound helper still passes. `identity_excludes_arrival` only proves an unused parameter irrelevant; it must cover the actual identity function for the same schedule, generation and scheduled instant. Prove the selected bounded invariant for the production composed classify-and-step transition for each policy from admissible bounded states/configurations (or prove and compose the necessary classifier/step lemmas with their preconditions in Cure); report precisely what quantifiers and assumptions Cure checks. A finite model trace does not fill this proof requirement. Include independent negative controls that mutate the actual production classification/bound and identity computation and establish that the corresponding checks fail, rather than only mutate SkipOverlap handling.

**REQUIRED 4 — Watermark, recorded decisions and concurrency need a coherent transition Contract.** The proposal says every decided slot advances the watermark and emits a decision, while laws `skip_overlap_keeps_state`, `queue_full_keeps_state`, and `duplicate_keeps_state` assert whole-state equality; the first two conflict whenever a new skipped slot advances the watermark. Distinguish unchanged unsettled set from watermark and emitted result, and prove or test the actual behavior. Define strict ordering, duplicate/equal timestamp handling, unseen earlier slots, initial watermark, future timestamps and redefinition/watermark scope; validate or reject invalid batches rather than silently discarding a legitimate out-of-order slot. A watermark alone is safe only when the caller supplies a complete ordered sequence with the stated boundary. State retention/restart is a caller assumption, not built-in durability. Pure independently evaluated concurrent activations each can produce the same occurrence: deterministic IDs alone do not serialize state or atomically deduplicate enqueue. Require serialized state transition/commit or a named atomic host boundary; the acceptance test must exercise two activations through that boundary or precisely show sequential replay and scope its conclusion to that condition, with no unsupported concurrent or exactly-once claim.

Explain the key encoding, payload equality/validation and explicit adapter/consumer ownership, and test the claimed mapping or narrow the composition statement. The shared envelope schema carries delivery results, not arbitrary occurrence decisions by mere assertion. No unused source dependencies are needed solely because a conceptual caller composes a Piece, but declare actual dependencies when source/API used and do not claim checked composition without an executable boundary test.
