## Lessons

- Higher-order Cure arguments are curried at the Elixir boundary; exported
  MFA calls retain their ordinary arity. The bundle tests preserve this check.
- Multi-root imports need source_roots for audit and qualified nullary type
  names to avoid migration warnings. See docs/cure-notes.md.
- Cure generated stdlib caches retain source identities: rebuild them after
  moving checkouts; do not treat copied caches as cold-install evidence.
