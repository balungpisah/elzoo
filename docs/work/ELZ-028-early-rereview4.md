| B1 | **REWORD.** The sampled rule transparently permits a renewal to become visible after expiry, which is a substantive qualification of live-only renewal. The claimed sample linearization contradicts unlocked reader observations. Replace the complete proposed after text with: <br><br>“`renew` extends only a lease that is live at the store-clock sample taken under the key's head lock within the transaction recording the change; a renewal issued before a handoff and evaluated after the handoff cannot revive the prior lease. A renewal sampled before expiry may commit after expiry; an unlocked status or hold may report the prior lease stale before that commit and report the renewed lease active afterwards. A renewal sampled at or after expiry is refused and records nothing. The same sampled-time rule applies to the active holder's re-claim; a reader reports a recorded stamp later than its own sample as ambiguous.” <br><br>Apply only if the coordinator expressly accepts that qualification and reconciles G1/G2; do not call the sample the combined object's linearization point. |

Overlapping invocations may both return the same key only when the earlier grant has ceased to be live before the later grant, and then the grants carry different record-revision fences; their result sets are not necessarily disjoint.” <br><br>Define clock-regression, release and seizure semantics consistently with the chosen admission rule, and reconcile the unqualified acceptance checkbox with this deliberate change. |
