# ELZ-366 — A tenant's subscription to a plan version: UTC monthly or yearly periods from an anchor, a trial, renewal with the period's fee line and grant, changes at the next period, cancel at period end or now

- Phase: Wave 9 Level 1
- Dependencies: ELZ-031, ELZ-033, ELZ-270, ELZ-285, ELZ-300
- Release: V2
- Output: new `domain/plan` Piece
- Change class: Piece-only
- Owned paths: `pieces/domain/plan/**` and its own catalogue/release-plan rows and generated views only. Do not edit root/shared code, another Piece (including `domain/money`, `domain/entitlement`, `domain/invoice` and `state/tenant-isolation`), `mix.exs`, `mix.lock` or CI.

## Outcome and boundary

One durable Piece over a consumer-owned Ecto Repo on PostgreSQL 16, with its period rule in Cure: a tenant's **subscription** to one plan version at a time, with a fee of one `domain/money` amount per period, periods that are UTC calendar months or years counted from an **anchor** and clamped to the month's last day, an optional trial, a renewal that advances exactly one period when it is due and returns that period's **fee line** and its **grant** for `domain/entitlement`, a plan change that takes effect at the next renewal, and a cancel at period end or now. The Contract source is the `domain/plan` section of [the Wave 9 plan](../docs/plans/bot-platform-wave9.md) ("Level 1"), under owner answer Q11 A (2026-11-24; "Owner answers (2026-11-24)" there) and the owner's plan-types decision of 2026-11-21 ("Subscriptions wait for Wave 9 (`domain/plan`, `domain/invoice`)").

The Piece prices nothing beyond the declared fee, writes no grant, invoice or journal row, and has no timer: the host calls `renew/3` with its trusted time (for example from a `state/scheduler` tick), stores the grant facts that it returns in its `domain/entitlement` facts, and puts the fee line on a `domain/invoice` invoice. No proration in this version (Q11 A).

The Piece keeps durable state and serializes concurrent renewals, so this task keeps the local small gate and gets the full review of the coordinator guide ("Reviewer by risk"; `AGENTS.md` sections 5 and 6).

## The period rule (Cure)

A Cure module `Elzoo.PlanPeriod` holds the pure rule over civil dates: `days_in_month(year, month)` (the Gregorian rule: 29 in February of a year divisible by 4 and not by 100, or by 400), and `boundary(anchor_year, anchor_month, anchor_day, interval, k)` for `k ≥ 0`, the date that is `k` months (`interval` `Month`) or `12k` months (`Year`) after the anchor's month, with day `min(anchor_day, days_in_month(...))`. A boundary is always computed from the anchor, never from the boundary before it: a 31 January anchor gives 28 or 29 February, then 31 March. Elixir converts a Unix millisecond time to and from a UTC civil date and time of day with OTP `:calendar`; a boundary keeps the anchor's UTC time of day.

The selected laws are those that the pinned Cure checks: the leap-year cases (`days_in_month` of February 2024, 2100, 2000 and 2027 equal 29, 28, 29 and 28) and boundary cases over literals (anchor 31 January with `k` 1, 2 and 13; anchor 29 February 2024, yearly, `k` 1 and 4; anchor 15 March, `k` 0). The rule over every anchor day and month is test evidence: an exhaustive test over every anchor day 1..31 of every month, in a leap and a non-leap year, for `k` 0..24 monthly and 0..8 yearly, against an independent oracle written in the test: the year and month shifted by integer arithmetic on `year * 12 + month − 1`, and the day `min(anchor_day, Date.days_in_month(Date.new!(y, m, 1)))`. If the worker finds a law form that the pinned Cure checks for every day, it may state it as well and says so in the pull request.

## Interface

Module `Elzoo.Plan`. Every function after `new/1` takes the binding, the tenant and one request map.

- `Elzoo.Plan.Migration.install(prefix)` installs the tenant-isolation readers and one protected table, subscriptions, `PRIMARY KEY (tenant, subscription_id)`, with a unique partial index on `tenant` for rows whose state is not `canceled`. A bad `prefix` raises `ArgumentError` before any DDL.
- `new(opts)` takes exactly `repo`, `prefix` and optional `renew_ahead_ms` (0..86,400,000, default 0); `{:ok, binding}` or `{:error, :invalid}`. A period is due for renewal at `end_ms − renew_ahead_ms`, so the host can store the next period's grant before the boundary: that grant is not yet valid until `end_ms`, so it never overlaps the current one, and the tenant has no gap at the boundary. With 0 the host's renewal at `end_ms` decides the gap, which the README states. After a renewal ahead, the subscription's `period` is the next period (its `start_ms` is still in the future) until `end_ms` of the one before it passes.
- A **subscription** is `%{subscription_id, plan: %{plan_id, version}, fee, interval, anchor_ms, state, period, trial_end_ms, cancel_at_period_end, cancel_ms, pending_change, revision}`: `subscription_id` 1..100 bytes; `plan_id` 1..128 bytes and `version` 1..2^31−1 (the `domain/entitlement` plan identity); `fee` a non-negative `domain/money` amount; `interval` `:month` or `:year`; `state` `:trialing`, `:active`, `:past_due` or `:canceled`; `period` `%{index, start_ms, end_ms}` (index 0 is the trial, index `k ≥ 1` runs from boundary `k − 1` to boundary `k`); `pending_change` `nil` or `%{plan, fee}`; `cancel_ms` `nil` or the end that a period-end cancel fixed; `revision` from 1. Times are Unix milliseconds 0..2^63−1, compared only after conversion.
- A **fee line** is `%{subscription_id, period: %{index, start_ms, end_ms}, plan: %{plan_id, version}, amount}`; `domain/invoice` takes this shape. A **grant** is the `domain/entitlement` grant `%{grant_id: "<subscription_id>/<index>", tenant, plan_id, version, valid_from_ms: start_ms, valid_to_ms: end_ms, grace_until_ms: nil, revoked_at_ms: nil}`; a **revocation** is `%{grant_id, revoked_at_ms}`.
- `create(p, tenant, %{subscription_id, plan, fee, interval, start_ms, trial_end_ms})`: `trial_end_ms` `nil` or greater than `start_ms`. The anchor is `trial_end_ms`, or `start_ms` without a trial. With a trial the subscription is `:trialing` in period 0 (`start_ms` to the anchor) and the result is `{:ok, %{subscription, fee_line: nil, grants: [grant_of_period_0]}}`; without one it is `:active` in period 1 and the result holds that period's fee line and grant. Idempotent by `subscription_id`: equal bytes return the stored first result as `{:ok, ...}` with `duplicate: true`; other bytes are `:conflict`; a tenant that holds a subscription that is not `:canceled` gets `:subscription_exists`.
- `renew(p, tenant, %{subscription_id, from_index, now_ms})`. Under the row lock:
  - the current period's index is `from_index`: for `cancel_at_period_end`, the renewal is `:not_due` until `now_ms ≥ cancel_ms`, the end that the cancel fixed (below; a cancel at period end is never taken ahead, so the running grant has ended when the subscription is canceled and a new subscription cannot overlap it), and then the subscription becomes `:canceled` and the result has no fee line and no grant; otherwise, when `now_ms < end_ms − renew_ahead_ms` it is `:not_due`; else the pending change, if any, becomes the plan and fee, the period advances exactly one (period `from_index + 1`, also from the trial), a `:trialing` subscription becomes `:active`, and the result holds the new period's fee line and grant;
  - the current index is `from_index + 1` and that period was made by a renewal from `from_index`: the stored result of that renewal again, with `duplicate: true`, and nothing written; when a cancel has since withdrawn that period, the repeat holds `fee_line: nil`, `grants: []` and that period's fee line as `withdrawn_fee_line`, so a retried renewal never bills a withdrawn period;
  - otherwise `:stale_period`.

  A renewal far past its due time still advances one period; the host calls again for the next one, so every period gets its fee line. It returns `{:ok, %{subscription, fee_line, grants, duplicate, withdrawn_fee_line}}`, `withdrawn_fee_line` `nil` except in that repeat.
- `change(p, tenant, %{subscription_id, expected_revision, plan, fee})` sets `pending_change` (no proration); `cancel(p, tenant, %{subscription_id, expected_revision, at: :period_end | :now, now_ms})`: `:period_end` sets `cancel_at_period_end` and fixes `cancel_ms`, the end of the period running at `now_ms`: the current period's `end_ms`, or, after a renewal ahead while the current period has not started (`now_ms < start_ms`), its `start_ms`, and then the result also holds the revocation of that period's grant at its `start_ms`; a `:period_end` cancel of a subscription whose `cancel_ms` is set keeps it and returns no new revocation or fee line; `:now` makes it `:canceled` and returns one revocation at `now_ms` for each grant of the subscription that is not yet expired at `now_ms` (the running period's and, after a renewal ahead, the next period's), with no refund. A cancel after a renewal ahead whose period has not started also returns that period's fee line as `withdrawn_fee_line`, which the host removes from what it bills (the period is never served); otherwise `withdrawn_fee_line` is `nil`. `cancel/3` returns `{:ok, %{subscription, revocations, withdrawn_fee_line}}`; `mark_past_due(p, tenant, %{subscription_id, expected_revision})` (from `:active`) and `mark_paid(p, tenant, %{subscription_id, expected_revision})` (from `:past_due` to `:active`) take the host's reading of the invoice. Each transition names the expected revision: another revision is `:stale_revision`; a transition not allowed from the state is `{:state, state}`; a canceled subscription takes none.
- `get(p, tenant, %{subscription_id})`; `verify(p)` checks the table with `Elzoo.TenantIsolation.verify/3`.

Errors are fixed atoms that never echo an ID: `:invalid`, `:in_transaction`, `:isolation`, `:conflict`, `:subscription_exists`, `:unknown_subscription`, `:not_due`, `:stale_period`, `:stale_revision`, `{:state, state}`, `:unavailable`.

## Rules

Writes check shape and bounds (`:invalid`), then `repo.in_transaction?()` (`:in_transaction`) before any query, then run in `Elzoo.TenantIsolation.transact/3` with the READ COMMITTED check; every statement passes `log: false`. `renew/3` and every transition take the subscription row with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`, so two concurrent renewals of one period commit one advance, and the other returns the duplicate. The row keeps the last renewal's result (its `from_index` and its fee line and grant) so that a repeat returns equal values, except after a cancel withdrew that period, when the repeat returns the withdrawn form of the renewal rule. A past-due subscription renews like an active one; when to cancel it is the host's dunning policy. Amounts are stored as their `Codec.encode/2` bytes.

`piece.json` declares telemetry and control `not_applicable`, the config of the binding, and the dependencies `state/tenant-isolation`, `domain/money` and `domain/entitlement` (the consumer of its grant shape; acceptance 6 calls `Elzoo.Entitlement.resolve/3`, and the copied consumer of a new Piece holds only its declared closure); the README states the minimum grants of the application role and that the host stores the returned grants.

## Acceptance

1. The period rule: the selected Cure laws compile and audit clean (no axioms, holes or unresolved, as the ready Cure Pieces audit theirs), and the exhaustive test of the rule section passes against its independent oracle.
2. Renewal at `end_ms − 1` is `:not_due` with nothing written, and at `end_ms` advances one period with its fee line and grant; with `renew_ahead_ms` 3,600,000 the same holds at `end_ms − 3,600,001` and `end_ms − 3,600,000`; a renewal of a 31 January anchor gives periods ending 28 February, 31 March and 30 April (2027), and of a 29 February 2024 yearly anchor 28 February 2025 and 29 February 2028.
3. A repeated renewal of the same `from_index` with no cancel since returns equal values with `duplicate: true` and writes nothing (the row's revision is unchanged; the withdrawn repeat is case 5's); a `from_index` two behind is `:stale_period`.
4. A trial: `:trialing` in period 0 with a grant and no fee line; the renewal at the trial's end makes it `:active` in period 1 with its fee line.
5. A change takes effect at the next renewal (the fee line and the grant carry the new plan and fee), not before; a cancel at period end ends it at the next renewal with no fee line, and with `renew_ahead_ms` above 0 that renewal is `:not_due` before `end_ms`; a cancel at period end inside the ahead window (after a renewal ahead) returns the revocation of the next grant at its `start_ms` and the next period's fee line as `withdrawn_fee_line`, the renewal is `:not_due` before that `start_ms` and cancels it at it, and `Elzoo.Entitlement.resolve/3` after the boundary is `:revoked`; a repeat of the ahead renewal after either ahead-window cancel returns no fee line and no grant, and the withdrawn fee line; a second `:period_end` cancel after that boundary keeps `cancel_ms`, and the renewal then cancels the subscription; a cancel now returns the revocation of the running grant, and a cancel now inside the ahead window (after a renewal ahead) returns two revocations and the next period's fee line as `withdrawn_fee_line`, so `Elzoo.Entitlement.resolve/3` at `now_ms` and after the boundary is `:revoked`; a canceled subscription refuses every transition, and the tenant may then create another.

## Limits to state

The trusted time, the storage of the returned grants and fee lines, and the past-due reading are the host's. UTC only; a period boundary keeps the anchor's UTC time of day. One active subscription per tenant. No proration, refund, tax, dunning, timer or invoice. The rule over every day is test evidence, not a proof, unless the pull request states a checked law. The `state/tenant-isolation` limits apply.
